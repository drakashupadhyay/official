About Dr. Akash Kumar Upadhyay

Dr. Akash Kumar Upadhyay is a Bangalore-based physiotherapist with over 15 years of hands-on clinical experience. He completed his Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) at Acharya Institute of Health Sciences / Acharya Medical College, Bangalore, and has since treated patients with sports injuries, post-surgical needs, spine and joint conditions, chronic pain and neurological conditions.

What sets his practice apart is a patient-first approach. He takes time to understand your condition, your goals and your daily routine before designing a plan around you. Wherever it is clinically appropriate, he works to restore mobility and relieve pain without surgery, and coordinates with orthopedic surgeons so every patient receives dependable, joined-up care.
